# AZRIEL — DM GUIDE

This character was built with hooks baked in.

*All things are yours to use, reshape, or ignore.*

## THE CHARACTER IN ONE BREATH

A goliath paladin of the Silver Flame, stolen from his mountain tribe as a child and forged into a templar. He was the sworn brother of a golden champion, **Aurelian**, who was corrupted by a whispering confessor serving the Shadow bound within the holy fire. At a public war-council, he drew his blade to cut down the tempter — and faltered for one second, and that hesitation became the final push that damned his brother to the dark. Cast out and branded **the Forsaken**, he now hunts evil while the Abyss itself whispers to him, wearing his brother's voice. He fights not to become the very thing they accused him of.

**His arc is Devotion → Vengeance,** and his ultimate fate — redemption or damnation — is deliberately **unwritten**, for you and the player to write together.

## THE TRIGGER-NAMES

Two names are keys to his heart. Used well, they land like blows.

- **GAVRIEL** — the name of the man he was before the fall. No one should use it casually; the party knows him only as Azriel. Hearing "Gavriel" always wounds. It belongs in the mouths of: the **Shadow / the Abyss** (whispering it to unmake him); **Aurelian**, his corrupted brother (with contempt or false warmth); and anyone from the **old order** who recognizes him.
- **TALOK** — his true, lost goliath name, buried when the Flame took him. The Abyss knows it and uses it to reach the child beneath the templar. Reclaiming this name could be a personal quest.

Deploy both deliberately. They are the sharpest tools in the box.

## BUILT-IN ANTAGONISTS


- **Aurelian** — his fallen sworn brother, now wholly **Tarnished**, who believes *Azriel* is the traitor who brought him low. He hunts Azriel across the world. Ideal as a recurring antagonist building toward a tragic confrontation. *His current state is left open — vanished into the Abyss, ruling a corrupted crusade, or presumed dead. Your call.*
- **Confessor Lucien** — patient agent of the Shadow, who wears the holy robes of a Confessor. He engineered Aurelian's fall and still lives. The stroke Azriel failed to land. A subtle, insidious recurring threat who hides in plain sight.
- **The Shadow in the Flame (Bel Shalor)** — the canon demon-overlord bound within the Silver Flame, whose whispers *are* the "Abyss" that tempts Azriel. The ultimate source of the corruption arc.

## MECHANICAL LEVERS

- **Cloud's Jaunt as a doorway.** His teleport is reskinned as *briefly stepping through the Abyss.* Narratively, the Shadow gets a whisper in every time he uses it — a natural hook for tempting him mid-combat, slipping a voice or a vision into the half-second he's "through."
- **Seraphiel, unwritten.** His endpoint name means "the burning one" and can resolve two ways: **redemption** (pure holy fire — he ascends to the name) or **damnation** (infernal fire — the Abyss claims him). Treat his major moral choices as steps toward one pole or the other.

## THE RECURRING TEST

His defining wound is **the hesitation** — he drew his blade and paused, and the pause damned everyone he loved. The single most resonant thing you can do with this character is to place him, again, in a moment where he must *choose to strike or stay his hand,* decisively, under pressure, with stakes.

## SETTING NOTE

The Church of the Silver Flame is an Eberron institution.

- **The Silver Flame** → a holy-fire order devoted to burning out corruption, under a Realms deity such as **Torm** (duty & loyalty, the paladin's god), **Helm** (the vigilant guardian), or **Lathander** (dawn & renewal).
- **The Shadow in the Flame (Bel Shalor)** → a demon or fiend bound within that order's sacred fire, whispering to the faithful in the voice of their god.
- **The Tarnished** → whatever that order names its fallen.
- Everything else — the brotherhood, the confessor's poison, the war-council, the oath — carries over untouched.
