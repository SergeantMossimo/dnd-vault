## DAMAGE TYPE QUICK KEY
- **Slashing** — physical damage from blades (your Greatsword & Handaxe). Common; few things resist it.
- **Radiant** — holy/light damage (your Divine Smite). Rarely resisted; **fiends & undead are extra vulnerable** to your smite.
- **Fire** — from Searing Smite. Note: fairly commonly resisted by some monsters.

---

## WEAPON MASTERY PROPERTIES
- **GRAZE** *(Greatsword)* — When you **MISS** an attack, you still deal damage equal to your STR modifier (**5 damage**). You never whiff completely. No roll needed — just deal 5 slashing.
- **VEX** *(Handaxe)* — When you **HIT**, you get **advantage on your next attack roll against that same target**. Great for setting up a Divine Smite hit.

---

## HOW TO ATTACK

**On your turn you can make ONE attack** (until level 5, when you get two).

### GREATSWORD (main weapon)
1. Roll **d20 + 7** to hit.
2. If you **hit** → roll **2d6 + 5** slashing damage.
3. If you **miss** → GRAZE: deal **5** slashing anyway.

### HANDAXE (backup / setup)
1. Roll **d20 + 7** to hit.
2. If you **hit** → roll **1d6 + 5** slashing.
3. VEX: your **next** attack vs. that target has **advantage**.
4. *(Can also be thrown — see box below.)*

### DIVINE SMITE (your signature move)
- Use it **right AFTER you HIT** with a melee attack (it's a **bonus action**).
- Adds **2d8 radiant** to that hit (**+1d8 = 3d8** if the target is a **fiend or undead**).
- **FREE once per long rest** (no slot). After that, costs one spell slot.
- Only once per turn. Best saved for a **big hit or a critical** (on a crit, the smite dice double!).


---

## HANDAXE — THROWN OPTION
- **Range:** 20 ft (normal) / 60 ft (long — disadvantage past 20).
- **To hit:** d20 + 7 · **Damage:** 1d6 + 5 slashing.
- VEX still applies on a hit. Good for flyers / fleeing enemies.

---

## HOW TO CAST YOUR SPELLS
You have **2 spell slots** (level 1). Spells regained on a **long rest**.
**CRM = Concentration / Ritual / Material** flags.

> You can hold **only ONE Concentration spell** at a time. Bless is concentration; Searing Smite and Divine Smite are NOT — so you can run Bless + smite freely.

### DIVINE SMITE — *always prepared (free), doesn't count against your prepared spells*
- **Casting time:** Bonus action, right after a melee hit
- **Range:** Self (the hit you just landed)
- **CRM:** none
- **Effect:** +2d8 radiant (+1d8 vs fiend/undead). Free 1×/long rest, then costs a slot.

### SEARING SMITE
- **Casting time:** Bonus action (before/with your attack)
- **Range:** Self
- **CRM:** none
- **Effect:** Your next hit deals **+1d6 fire**, and the target keeps taking **1d6 fire** at the start of its turns until it succeeds a CON save (DC 13) or someone heals it. Stacks fine with Bless.

### BLESS
- **Casting time:** Action
- **Range:** 30 ft · **Targets:** up to 3 creatures (you + allies)
- **CRM:** **Concentration** (up to 1 min); Material (a holy symbol — you have one)
- **Effect:** Each blessed creature adds **+1d4** to **attack rolls AND saving throws**. Turns misses into hits. Cast at the start of a big fight.

## SPECIAL ABILITIES

### LAY ON HANDS (healing pool = 5 HP)
- **Bonus action**, touch a creature (or yourself), restore HP from the pool (up to 5 total per long rest).
- Or spend **5 HP from the pool to cure the Poisoned condition**.
- Refills on a long rest.

### CLOUD'S JAUNT (Giant Ancestry) — 2 uses / long rest
- **Bonus action**, teleport up to **30 ft** to a space you can **see** (must be empty).
- Doesn't provoke opportunity attacks; ignores terrain.

### POWERFUL BUILD (Goliath)
- Advantage to escape a **Grapple**; count as one size larger for carrying capacity.

### TOUGH (feat)
- Passive: +2 HP per level (already baked into your 15 HP).

---

## BONUS-ACTION TRAFFIC JAM (important!)
These all want your **bonus action** — you get only ONE per turn, so choose:
- Divine Smite (after a hit)
- Searing Smite
- Lay on Hands
- Cloud's Jaunt

Plan your turn: usually **attack + one bonus-action choice + move**.
