## DAMAGE TYPE QUICK KEY
- **Slashing** — physical damage from blades (your Greatsword & Handaxe). Common; few things resist it.
- **Radiant** — holy/light damage (your Divine Smite). Rarely resisted; **fiends & undead are extra vulnerable** to your smite.
- **Fire** — from Searing Smite. Note: fairly commonly resisted by some monsters.

---

## WEAPON MASTERY PROPERTIES
- **GRAZE** *(Greatsword)* — When you **MISS** an attack, you still deal damage equal to your STR modifier (**5 damage**). You never whiff completely. No roll needed — just deal 5 slashing.
- **VEX** *(Handaxe)* — When you **HIT**, you get **advantage on your next attack roll against that same target**. Great for setting up a Divine Smite hit.

---

## FIGHTING STYLE — GREAT WEAPON FIGHTING
- When you roll damage with your Greatsword, treat any **1 or 2 on a damage die as a 3**. Smooths out low rolls on your 2d6. (Applies to the weapon dice, not smite dice.)

---

## HOW TO ATTACK

**On your turn you can make ONE attack** (until level 5, when you get two).

### GREATSWORD (main weapon)
1. Roll **d20 + 7** to hit.
2. If you **hit** → roll **2d6 + 5** slashing damage (treat 1s & 2s as 3s).
3. If you **miss** → GRAZE: deal **5** slashing anyway.

### HANDAXE (backup / setup)
1. Roll **d20 + 7** to hit.
2. If you **hit** → roll **1d6 + 5** slashing.
3. VEX: your **next** attack vs. that target has **advantage**.
4. *(Can also be thrown — see box below.)*

### DIVINE SMITE (your signature move)
- Use it **right AFTER you HIT** with a melee attack (it's a **bonus action**).
- Adds **2d8 radiant** to that hit (**+1d8 = 3d8** if the target is a **fiend or undead**).
- **FREE once per long rest** (no slot). After that, costs one spell slot.
- Only once per turn. Best saved for a **big hit or a critical** (on a crit, the smite dice double!).

---

## HANDAXE — THROWN OPTION
- **Range:** 20 ft (normal) / 60 ft (long — disadvantage past 20).
- **To hit:** d20 + 7 · **Damage:** 1d6 + 5 slashing.
- VEX still applies on a hit. Good for flyers / fleeing enemies.

## OATH OF VENGEANCE FEATURES

### CHANNEL DIVINITY — 2 uses / short rest
Spend a use to fuel one of the options below. Regain on a **short or long rest**.

- **VOW OF ENMITY** — When you take the Attack action, mark a creature within **30 ft**. You have **Advantage on all attacks against it for 1 min**. If it drops to 0 HP, move the vow to a new creature within 30 ft (free, no action). *This is your bread-and-butter — mark your prey, then smite it with advantage (more hits = more crits = bigger smites).*
- **DIVINE SENSE** — Action: sense **fiends, undead & celestials within 60 ft** (location & type) until the end of your next turn.

### OATH SPELLS (always prepared — don't count against prepared limit)
- **Hunter's Mark** and **Bane** (see spell section below).

---

## HOW TO CAST YOUR SPELLS
You have **4 spell slots** (level 1). Spells regained on a **long rest**.
**CRM = Concentration / Ritual / Material** flags.

> You can hold **only ONE Concentration spell** at a time. Bless, Hunter's Mark, and Bane are all Concentration — pick one. Searing Smite and Divine Smite are NOT, so they run alongside a concentration spell freely.

### DIVINE SMITE — *always prepared (free), doesn't count against prepared spells*
- **Casting time:** Bonus action, right after a melee hit · **Range:** Self
- **CRM:** none
- **Effect:** +2d8 radiant (+1d8 vs fiend/undead). Free 1×/long rest, then costs a slot.

### HUNTER'S MARK *(Oath — always prepared)*
- **Casting time:** Bonus action · **Range:** 90 ft
- **CRM:** **Concentration** (up to 1 hr)
- **Effect:** Mark a creature; deal **+1d6** damage to it whenever you hit it. If it dies, move the mark to a new target (bonus action). Great on your Vow of Enmity target.

### BANE *(Oath — always prepared)*
- **Casting time:** Action · **Range:** 30 ft · **Targets:** up to 3 creatures
- **CRM:** **Concentration** (up to 1 min); Material
- **Effect:** Failed CHA save → targets subtract **1d4** from their attack rolls AND saving throws.

### SEARING SMITE
- **Casting time:** Bonus action (before/with your attack) · **Range:** Self
- **CRM:** none
- **Effect:** Your next hit deals **+1d6 fire**, and the target keeps taking **1d6 fire** at the start of its turns until it succeeds a CON save (DC 13) or is healed.

### BLESS
- **Casting time:** Action · **Range:** 30 ft · **Targets:** up to 3 creatures (you + allies)
- **CRM:** **Concentration** (up to 1 min); Material (holy symbol)
- **Effect:** Each blessed creature adds **+1d4** to **attack rolls AND saving throws**.

*(Plus one more prepared 1st-level spell of your choice — check your sheet.)*

## SPECIAL ABILITIES

### LAY ON HANDS (healing pool = 15 HP)
- **Bonus action**, touch a creature (or yourself), restore HP from the pool (up to 15 total per long rest).
- Or spend **5 HP from the pool to cure the Poisoned condition**.
- Refills on a long rest.

### CLOUD'S JAUNT (Giant Ancestry) — 2 uses / long rest
- **Bonus action**, teleport up to **30 ft** to a space you can **see** (must be empty).
- Doesn't provoke opportunity attacks; ignores terrain.
- **Uses = your Proficiency Bonus.** So **2/long rest now**. Grows to 3 at level 5, etc.
- **RP — the crossing ritual** (he steps *through the Abyss* when he jaunts):
  - Entering: **"Ka'thelua, oren im."** [kah-thel-OO-ah, OR-en eem] — *"Light, guide me, one more step."*
  - Emerging: **"Aelith na Talok."** [AY-lith nah TAH-lok] — *"My name is still Talok."*

### POWERFUL BUILD (Goliath)
- Advantage to escape a **Grapple**; count as one size larger for carrying capacity.

### TOUGH (feat)
- Passive: +2 HP per level (already baked into your HP).

---

## BONUS-ACTION TRAFFIC JAM (important!)
These all want your **bonus action** — you get only ONE per turn, so choose:
- Divine Smite (after a hit)
- Searing Smite
- Lay on Hands
- Cloud's Jaunt
- Hunter's Mark (to cast/move it)

*Note: Vow of Enmity does NOT use your bonus action (it's free with your Attack), so you can Vow **and** still smite the same turn.*

Plan your turn: usually **attack + one bonus-action choice + move**.
