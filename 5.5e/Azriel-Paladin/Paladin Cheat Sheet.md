## PROPERTY & MASTERY GLOSSARY

| Term | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Graze** *(Greatsword)* | On a **miss**, still deal 5 damage (STR mod). No roll. |
| **Vex** *(Handaxe)* | On a **hit**, advantage on your next attack vs that target. |
| **Sap** *(Duality, 2H)* | On a **hit**, target has **disadvantage on its next attack**. |
| **Finesse** *(Duality, 1H)* | Use STR *or* DEX. Yours is STR (+5). |
| **Versatile** | One- or two-handed; bigger die in two hands. |
| **Thrown** | Can be thrown; uses same attack mod as melee. |
| **Heavy / Two-Handed** *(Greatsword)* | Needs both hands; qualifies for Great Weapon Fighting. |
| **Light** *(Handaxe)* | Small/quick; enables dual-wielding. |
| **Great Weapon Fighting** | Two-handed weapon damage: treat **1s & 2s as 3s**. |

## MELEE OPTIONS

### GREATSWORD — 2H
- **Hit:** d20 **+7** · **Dmg:** **2d6 + 5** slashing (1s/2s→3s)
- **Miss:** GRAZE — deal **5** anyway

### DUALITY (longsword) — 1H
- **Hit:** d20 **+7** · **Dmg:** **1d8 + 5** slashing
- **Finesse** (STR or DEX — use STR)

### DUALITY (longsword) — 2H
- **Hit:** d20 **+7** · **Dmg:** **1d10 + 5** slashing (1s/2s→3s)
- **SAP** — target has disadvantage on its next attack
- ⚠️ **Bonus Xd6** — DM tells you the count. *Ask what triggers it.*

### HANDAXE — melee
- **Hit:** d20 **+7** · **Dmg:** **1d6 + 5** slashing
- **VEX** — advantage on your next attack vs that target

### UNARMED STRIKE
- **Hit:** d20 **+7** · **Dmg:** **6** bludgeoning (1 + STR)
- Or instead: **Grapple** or **Shove** (DC 15 save)

### DIVINE SMITE (after a hit)
- **Bonus action**, right after you **HIT** in melee
- **+2d8 radiant** (**3d8** vs fiend/undead)
- **Free 1×/long rest**, then costs a slot. Once per turn.
- **Crit doubles the dice** — save it for crits.

- **Range:** 20 / 60 ft (disadv. past 20)

## OATH OF VENGEANCE

### CHANNEL DIVINITY — 2 uses / short rest
- **VOW OF ENMITY** — With your Attack action, mark a creature within 30 ft: **advantage on all attacks vs it, 1 min**. Free move to a new target if it drops. *Does NOT use your bonus action.*
- **DIVINE SENSE** — Action: sense fiends/undead/celestials within 60 ft until end of next turn.

## SPECIAL ABILITIES

### LAY ON HANDS — pool = 20 HP
- **Bonus action**, touch a creature (or yourself).
- Heal any amount from the pool, or spend **5** to cure **Poisoned**.
- Refills on a long rest.

### CLOUD'S JAUNT — 2 uses / long rest
- **Bonus action**, teleport **30 ft** to a space you can see.
- No opportunity attacks; ignores terrain.
- **In:** "Ka'thelua, oren im." [kah-thel-OO-ah, OR-en eem] — *Light, guide me.*
- **Out:** "Aelith na Talok." [AY-lith nah TAH-lok] — *My name is still Talok.*

### INSPIRING LEADER (feat)
- 10 min speech → you + up to **5 allies** gain **7 temp HP** each.
- (CHA mod + paladin level. Temp HP doesn't stack — reapply as needed.)

### POWERFUL BUILD (Goliath)
- Advantage to escape a **Grapple**.
- Count as one size larger for carrying capacity.

### TOUGH (feat)
- +2 HP per level — already included in your 42.
