The Oath of Moment

By the Flame that forged me, I am bound.
By the light I swore to carry, I do not turn.

I hear the Abyss. It sings in a voice I know —
it wears the faces I have loved, it calls me by the name I have lost,
and it promises an end to the burden.
But I am the vow the dark cannot unmake.
Let it pull. Let it whisper. Let it wear my dead like masks —
it will not have me while I draw breath.

And should the Flame within me gutter to ash,
then I shall become the ash, and burn on.

This I swear, with my god as my witness.
Not while I draw breath.
